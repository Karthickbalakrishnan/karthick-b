Insights
Four insights changed the direction of the experience
01

More results created decision fatigue.

Both personas spent more time interpreting result sets than acting on them.

02

Duplicates eroded trust before content did.

Version drift made it harder to identify the authoritative document, especially under RBAC.

03

Context is the platform, not just the query.

Relevant filters and answer formats change by hardware family — Kiyo CX differs from SABRE FX.

04

A desktop answer isn't a cleanroom answer.

Field Supervisors needed a 3-second numeric tolerance; Engineers needed full revision lineage.

Strategy
From repository-centric search to intent-centric discovery
REPOSITORY-CENTRIC
Where is the information?
Search→Filters→Documents→Compare
INTENT-CENTRIC
What does this role need, on this platform, right now?
Intent→Platform context→Intelligence→Relevant knowledge→Action
Make the system understand the role, the platform, and the intent.
Strategic reflections
What this project taught me
01

Cognitive ergonomics is the real design surface.

The database was never the constraint — the working memory load of scanning, comparing, and validating was.

02

Domain taxonomy has to be first-class, not decorative.

Product families, revision lineage, and document categories aren't metadata — they're the vocabulary the system reasons in.

03

Security and trust are design decisions, not IT afterthoughts.

RBAC framed as a justification workflow, not a wall, kept the experience both compliant and usable.
