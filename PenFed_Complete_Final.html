Business problem & research plan

Every stage had a hidden drop-off. The real opportunity was to reduce uncertainty.

The business issue was not simply “too much data.” It was missing clarity at the moments when the applicant had to make a decision. The platform surfaced document requirements, status updates, and policy details, but without a coherent emotional or cognitive flow.

Review
Key findings

The issue was not “more content.” It was “less confidence.”

What users struggled with
  • Understanding whether the app was actively validating inputs.
  • Making sense of the available rate and term combinations.
  • Knowing which documents were necessary before they were requested.
  • Finding emotional confidence in the next step after a form interaction.
Design responses
  • Progressive disclosure for loan options and required documents.
  • Stateful status messaging that explains validation in plain language.
  • Prioritized “why this matters” copy near each decision point.
  • One clear next-step view at each stage.
“The problem wasn’t a lack of information; it was a lack of meaning in the moment of decision.” Research synthesis, round 3
Design framework

Three design principles shaped the redesign.

01

Reduce ambiguity

Every step should tell the user whether the system is doing something, waiting on something, or asking them to act.

02

Clarify decision stakes

Rates, options, and smart defaults needed plain-language explanation near the moment of selection.

03

Orient the next move

Every screen needed a singular “what happens next?” signal, supported by context and reassurance.

Before

  • Dense form layout
  • Minimal status guidance
  • Hidden validation logic

After

  • Progressive disclosure
  • Real-time state language
  • Priority decision cues

Outcome

  • Reduced friction
  • Faster completion
  • Higher confidence

Strategic recommendation

The product should prioritize clarity over density: fewer states, more meaningful labels, and stronger confirmation cues. Mortgage UX works best when the user feels progress, not pressure.
